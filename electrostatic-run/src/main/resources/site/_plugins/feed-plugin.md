## What it is for

`FeedPlugin` produces a syndication feed from indexed content.

## Inputs

- Indexed content items from the content model.

## Output behavior

- Generates `feed.xml` in site output.
- Keeps feed entries aligned with generated post/index content.

## Why it matters

Feeds make new content discoverable in feed readers and automation workflows.
